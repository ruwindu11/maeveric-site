// Cross-departmental work

Cross-departmental work, end to end.

Every business event today touches multiple systems and the people who run them. maeveric runs the whole chain start to finish.

Trigger

A bank deposit notice lands in your finance inbox. USD 24,800 from Contoso Manufacturing, against an overdue invoice.

Context

Contoso has a new order on credit hold. The overdue balance pushed them past their limit.

Finance Agent

Matches the payment. Proposes the cash application and clears the overdue flag.

Sales Agent

Sees the credit is restored. Proposes releasing the held order and notifying the account manager.

Outcome

All systems in sync. Full audit trail. If any step fails, every prior step rolls back.

// Frequently asked

Questions buyers ask first.

How is maeveric different from Copilot?
Copilot summarises. maeveric runs workflows. Both can sit on the same desktop.
Does the AI post to my general ledger directly?
No. Every posting waits for a human approval inside MS Teams. Deterministic code does the actual commit.
Where does my data live?
Inside your own Azure tenant. Your Entra identity. Your Business Central. Nothing leaves it.
What happens if a workflow step fails halfway?
Every prior step rolls back. You never end up with a half-finished state across systems.
Can maeveric connect to our custom systems?
Yes, through the same MCP-based pattern we use for Business Central. New connectors take days, not months.
How long does a deployment take?
Usually a couple of weeks for the first domain, days for each additional domain. Most of the work is configuration, not custom code.
